Symbian apps, on a home screen that feels like your iPhone.

Halcyon runs Symbian^3 and Belle apps and games on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Install a .sis file with the + button, and it lands on the home screen next to the phone's own apps, ready to open.

The + button installs apps

In Halcyon, + opens the file picker. Choose a .sis or .sisx file and the app installs into the emulated phone, then appears on the next free spot on the home screen.

What it does

Like running Windows apps on a Mac with Parallels: the phone runs underneath, and each app feels like it belongs on your device.

A real home screen

Pages, a dock, drag to rearrange and long-press menus. Icons come from your phone's firmware, so Clock, Calendar and Messaging look the way they did.

Icons on your iPhone and Mac

Put any Symbian app on the iPhone Home Screen, or make it a Mac app in Launchpad and the Dock. Tapping it opens straight into that app.

Widgets and Spotlight

A phone clock, your dock apps and one-tap app widgets. Installed Symbian apps show up in Spotlight, Siri and the Shortcuts app.

JIT when you want speed

The Mac app always runs the Dynarmic JIT. On iPhone, launch Halcyon from StikDebug to switch from the interpreter to JIT.

Full Symbian mode

Prefer the whole phone? One setting hides the home screen and gives the emulated phone the entire window.

Free and open source

GPLv3, built on the EKA2L1 emulator. No accounts, no tracking, and nothing leaves your device.

Get started

  1. Dump your phone's firmware

    Halcyon needs the ROM and RPKG from a Symbian phone you own. The guide shows how. Halcyon never includes or downloads Nokia software.

  2. Install Halcyon

    On iPhone or iPad, add the source to SideStore or AltStore, or install the IPA directly. On a Mac, open the DMG and drag Halcyon to Applications.

  3. Add the firmware

    On first launch, choose the ROM and RPKG files. The phone boots and its own apps fill the home screen.

  4. Install your apps

    Tap + and pick .sis or .sisx files, or use Open in Halcyon from Files, Safari or Mail.
